For Module 3, Gaby Saboia and I wrote a 27-page recommendation report on the “Parking Spaces” section of the UTRGV Parking Services website, where every lot’s occupancy bar is green whether the lot is empty or full. We surveyed 11 students and faculty about the existing page, prototyped two redesigns from their answers, and recommended a compact lot panel.
Our biggest challenge was time. We had proposed timed usability audits and interviews, but ran a survey instead, which people could answer on their phones. We also could not test our prototypes with commuters before the deadline, so we weighted our decision matrix by how much each problem affected our respondents, and said so in the report.
The choice that worked best was designing for a specific person. Our persona, Sam, has red-green color blindness, which made the all-green bar a real barrier and led us to state each lot’s status in words. Leading with counts worked too: “8 of 9 have driven to a lot the page showed as available” made the case on its own.
