For Module 3, Gaby Saboia and I wrote a 27-page recommendation report on the “Parking Spaces” section of the UTRGV Parking Services website. We surveyed 11 students and faculty about the existing page, prototyped two redesigns from the survey, and built a recommendation report with a final design recommendation targeting the flaws of the current site.
Our biggest challenge was time. We had proposed timed usability audits and interviews, but ran a survey instead, so people could answer on their phones at their own pace. We also could not test our prototypes with commuters before the deadline, so we weighted our decision matrix by how much each problem affected our respondents, and said so in the report.
The choice that worked best was designing for accessibility. The current page shows availability through a green bar, which users with red-green color blindness cannot read, so our design states each lot's status in words as well as colors.
