What Technical Communication Means to Me Now
At the start of the semester, I believed technical communication changed with the audience. With peers it means accurately articulating my work, and with people outside the industry it means making my ideas accessible. I wanted to get better at the second.
Now I think accuracy and accessibility can work together and be implemented into my writing in a way any reader should be able to use what I wrote without requiring further elaboration. Writing a Docker instruction manual best exemplifies that idea. It helped me identify that things I find obvious can completely stop readers from making progress.
