For Module 1, I analyzed a mass email that the computer science department chair sent to every student and faculty member. The chair sent this email to the entirety of the department the same day early registration opened. I then wrote my own professional email to Dr. Paudel, introducing myself and asking a question about the Module 2 assignment.
The most difficult part of this assignment was altering the email sample itself. Every personal identifier was required to be redacted without losing the course codes and UTRGV system names my analysis depended on. I ended up keeping what was already published in the course catalog and advising material. The chair’s email also made me reflect on how authority or position gives you credibility. The email opened with “Hello Computer Science!” and proceeded to define none of its nomenclature but is an email I believe to be good communication. I say this because every recipient already belongs to the department and is expected to be familiar with the terms used.
In my own email, the choice that worked best was stating my three purposes in the first paragraph and giving each one a heading, so Dr. Paudel could jump straight to the question.
