Liam Reeves is a senior majoring in computer science at the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. He is dedicated to the study of deep learning and in building AI automation tools. He is currently researching how hidden prompt injections in documents can manipulate large language models, and he is the product owner for Shortlist, an internship-tracking platform where users can track internships with friends, their community, or by themselves, and where important information is extracted from each posting for them.
Liam is applying for internships at national labs, REUs, and in the industry, where both accurate and accessible communication are highly sought-after skills. In those roles, he plans to write documentation that does not depend on him being there to explain it, a lesson he learned writing a Docker instruction manual. After graduating, he plans to apply to the M.S. in Computer Science at UTRGV and pursue research, where his findings will need to work for both peers and readers outside his field.
